## What would flip a no

- A fair one-off price for the bottle with no plan (around $35 shipped) and filters I can buy one at a time without a subscription.
- If my current bottle broke and the creator showed a real side-by-side test proving Claro filters better than my LARQ or Brita, with faster US shipping.
- Independent certification (like NSF/ANSI) listing what the filter removes, plus real reviews from people who aren't paid creators
- Independent lab test results showing what the filter removes, plus seeing the creator actually use it on a trip for a while, not just a single unboxing.
- Published independent lab results showing what the filter removes, plus a fair one-time price for the bottle with free shipping and spare filters I can buy without a plan.
- Several hundred verified reviews plus independent lab test results on the filter, ideally sold somewhere like Amazon where I can read them.
- Free shipping on the bottle by itself with no subscription, and filters I can buy one at a time whenever I need them, at around $40 all in.
- If the bottle with a couple of filters came in around $35 total with free shipping and no subscription, so I could just reorder filters when I actually need them.
- Free shipping on the bottle by itself with no subscription, plus a real launch discount of 20% or more, so I'd get it for about $35-40 and buy filters only when I need them.
- My LARQ breaking or getting lost, plus independent lab test results on the filter and real reviews from people who aren't paid to post about it.
- The bottle on its own for around $25 with free shipping and no subscription, plus real reviews showing the filter actually makes tap water taste better.
- If my current bottle broke and I could get the Claro bottle alone for around $30 with free shipping and no plan attached.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
