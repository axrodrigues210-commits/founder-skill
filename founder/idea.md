# Claro

- What it is: An online (Shopify) direct-to-consumer brand selling a filtered water bottle, with replacement filters sold on subscription.
- Who it is for: NOT YET DEFINED by the founder. Implied by the plan: everyday reusable-bottle buyers in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand and the EU who would otherwise consider LARQ, reached through social media creators. Open question for `/founder-consumer`.
- What it sells, at what price (EU, EUR, VAT incl.; US$ in brackets):
  - Bottle, one-time: €50 ($49.99). Bottle with any filter plan: €40 ($39.99).
  - Single filter: €18 one-time ($17.99); €15.30 on a plan, one every 2 months ($15.29).
  - 3-pack of filters: €35 one-time ($34.99); €28 on a plan, every 6 months ($27.99).
  - The 3-pack plan is the one the founder wants people to pick (about 39% cheaper per month than the single plan).
- Where and how: Online only. Bottles from supplier Coolway (US$8.90 each at 1,000 units), filters US$2.20 each. Fulfilment and shipping by FF Logistics (3PL shipping from China, DDP, 4-8 working days). Bottle + plan and all renewals ship free; one-time orders pay flat shipping. Marketing: social media influencers/creators, each with a code; the customer gets 5% off their first order and the creator earns 15% commission on net sales from first-time purchases on their code. Light Meta ads assumed alongside.
- Budget and constraints: First batch of 1,000 bottles; upfront stock spend about US$10,480. Markets and assumed sales mix: US 25%, NZ 25%, AU 20%, EU 15%, UK 10%, CA 5%. Fixed costs about US$120/month (Shopify + subscription app). A Shopify store with live prices already exists.
