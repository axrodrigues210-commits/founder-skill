# Board brief: Claro (filtered water bottle + filter subscription)

Everything below comes from the founder and from the founder's own spreadsheet model ("claro_pricing_model_and_forecast_EU.xlsx"). Items marked ASSUMPTION are the founder's own unverified assumptions as labelled in that model.

## The idea
An online (Shopify) direct-to-consumer brand, Claro, selling a filtered water bottle. Replacement filters are sold on subscription: either a single filter every 2 months, or a 3-pack every 6 months. Marketing is through social media influencers/creators, who each get a code; a customer using a code gets 5% off, and the creator earns 15% commission on net sales (after discount, excl. shipping/tax) for that customer's FIRST purchase only (not renewals).

## Target customer
Not yet defined by the founder. The plan implies everyday reusable-bottle buyers in six markets (US, CA, UK, AU, NZ, EU) who might otherwise buy LARQ, reached via social creators.

## Product and supply
- Bottle from supplier Coolway: US$8.90/unit at 1,000 units (US$9.40 under 1,000). Boxed bottle 0.5 kg.
- Filter: US$2.20/unit. Supplier claims 150 L capacity per filter (Coolway's own test; no lab report yet). Advertised life: 2 months.
- Whether the bottle is insulated is not stated.
- Fulfilment: FF Logistics (3PL), ships DDP to door from its warehouse, 4-8 working days; EU delivery time TBC. Handling US$0.88/order; freight per kg + per order (e.g. bottle parcel US$7.60 to EU, US$9.70 to US; filter mailer about US$4).

Also priced in CAD, GBP, AUD, NZD. Bottle + plan and every renewal ship free. One-time bottle orders pay ~US$12.99 shipping; one-time filter orders ~US$6.99. On the 3-pack plan, the first 3-pack is charged and shipped with the bottle on day 0.

## Competitor reference (founder's own research)
LARQ (US): 25 oz insulated Flip Top bottle US$65 (US$54.95 with refill plan); Essential filter 3-pack US$35 one-time, US$29.95 on a 6-month plan; filter rated 94 L or 2 months. LARQ sells no single-filter plan. Claro undercuts LARQ's bottle by ~23% and its 3-pack plan by ~7%; one-time 3-pack is level. Other competitors (Brita, Water-to-Go, etc.) have not been mapped.

## Founder's unit economics (US$, before ads; US market)
- Bottle only: profit $38.30 (61% margin on net revenue).
- Bottle + 3-pack plan first order: $35.13 (52%).
- 3-pack renewal: $14.30 (51%).
- Bottle + single plan first order: $28.53 (52%).
- Single-filter renewal: $7.19 (47%; 34-45% in CA/AU/NZ/UK).
Costs include COGS, packaging, FF handling and freight, card fees (2.9% + $0.30) and a 4% refund allowance on first orders.

## Founder's 12-month P&L for the first 1,000 bottles (US$)
- Assumed split: 720 bottle-only, 200 bottle + 3-pack plan, 80 bottle + single plan. ASSUMPTION.
- Retention: 85% of 3-pack subscribers renew at month 6; 70% of single-filter subscribers keep going at each 2-monthly renewal. ASSUMPTION.
- Ad cost: US$15 per bottle sold (= $15,000). ASSUMPTION ("light Meta ads plus some creator costs").
- 25% of first orders use a creator code. ASSUMPTION. Creator cost total ~$3,090 (discount ~$800 + commission ~$2,290).
- Net revenue $71,310; contribution $40,700 (57%); after ads, creators and fixed costs: NET PROFIT ~$20,970 (29.7%).
- Upfront stock spend ~$10,480. Bottles to sell to recover stock + fixed costs: ~369.
- Year-2 forecast (2,000 bottles, assumed 2× year 1): net profit ~$45,150; ~500 active 3-pack subscribers at year end.

## Budget and constraints
First batch 1,000 bottles. Fixed costs ~US$120/month. Shopify store already live. No stated marketing budget beyond the $15/bottle assumption; no stated founder time or team.

## Open numbers nobody has yet
Real customer acquisition cost through creators; what creators actually require (flat fees, free product, commission rate); real conversion and retention; market size; whether the 150 L filter claim and any water-quality claims are certified.
